Halo · Control Center

A quieter surface for quick changes.

Keep the controls people reach for up front. Give network and audio details a bounded place to open, while the bottom actions stay put.

Design notes

  • Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, Airplane, and Focus sit in one compact group with a written state; details open from their card.
  • Display and sound get wide, 28 px tracks with a clear thumb. The selected endpoint is a secondary row below Sound.
  • Detail lists have their own scroll viewport; panel height and footer actions remain stable as rows scroll.
  • Uses Halo's existing 4 px spacing, Segoe UI Variable, teal accent, 12/20 px radii, and light/dark palette direction.

Guidance: Apple toggles calls for clear labels and visually distinct states; Microsoft motion calls for connected transitions and consistent flyout behavior. Example data is illustrative; this preview does not change system settings.
